02 / PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

Five clear planes. Zero hidden trust boundaries.

Every component owns one narrow responsibility: clients stay untrusted presentation layers, PostgreSQL 18 and descriptor-pinned storage hold authoritative state, workers execute leased turns and two-phase tools, and OpenMettle’s own per-model proxy isolates and meters inference.

03 / SECURITY, PRIVACY & GOVERNANCE

Shared hardware shouldn’t mean shared secrets.

Running a model locally is only half the story. OpenMettle enforces multi-user isolation, four-point policy gates, and two-phase tool accountability across the database, identity boundary, run worker, and inference proxy.

S-01 ADR 0027 · ADR 0044

Per-User Chat Lockdown & Prefix-Cache Isolation

Every conversation, artifact, and memory record stays private to its creator by default, even inside shared projects; account administrators gain no implicit access to unshared items. Explicit sharing uses revocable grants with 404 concealment for hidden or out-of-scope targets.

At dispatch, openmettle-gateway rejects caller-supplied SGLang routing keys (cache_salt, extra_key, session_id, rid, lora_path) and injects a scoped cryptographic cache_salt (or per_request nonce mode) so KV prefix caches never bleed across users or private chats on qualified backends.

S-02 ADR 0044 · MIGRATION 0035

Four-Point Family & Study Protection Rules

IANA timezone- and DST-accurate quiet hours, study-hour model allowlists, and per-user or group model switches are evaluated by one authoritative PostgreSQL policy function, with optional user-level schedule overrides.

Rules run independently at all four checkpoints (API admission and preview, worker claim and step recheck, database round handoff, and proxy dispatch), failing closed whenever OIDC group evidence is stale, revoked, or missing.

S-03 ADR 0003 · 0022 · 0030 · 0044

Least-Privilege Database & Identity Isolation

Clean-room Rust services connect to PostgreSQL 18 through four disjoint, non-DDL, non-DELETE runtime roles: openmettle_api, dedicated session-issuing openmettle_issuance, openmettle_worker, and function-only openmettle_gateway, checked automatically on every migration.

Standards-based OIDC/SSO (PKCE, nonce, issuer/subject stability) pairs with local operator approval gates and one-time home invitation codes. Every inference round uses a single-use 32-byte bearer whose plaintext never touches PostgreSQL (only its SHA-256 digest is stored and checked).

S-04 ADR 0024 · 0035 · 0040 · 0041

Approvals, Pre-Round Quotas & Verifiable Auditability

Human-in-the-loop tool approvals bind cryptographically (SHA-256) to canonical arguments, tool version, exact destination, and live policy revisions; opt-in YOLO mode remains policy-gated and cannot bypass immutable human confirmation classes.

Exact-integer (numeric(38, 0)) transactional quotas hold worst-case pre-round token and micro-cost reservations and settle to measured usage from admission-pinned pricing, backed by configurable retention holds, a SHA-256 hash-chained deletion journal, and value-free, PII-free telemetry (OTLP export off by default).

04 / SERVING & PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

Cutting-edge inference meets dependable execution.

Fast local GPUs shouldn’t come at the cost of fragile chats, stranded VRAM, or leaky tools. OpenMettle unites AMD-first ROCm serving research with durable execution, traversal-proof storage, scoped tools, and clean multi-client APIs.

AMD-FIRST ROCm & RDNA4 · V1-18 · V1-19 · V1-21 · ADR 0042

Demand-Driven Elastic KV Cache & Curated SGLang Serving

Our curated SGLang serving stack (OpenMettle/sglang, openmettle-serving) pairs HIP virtual-memory Elastic KV Cache with FP8 attention caching, MXFP4 quantization, and speculative decoding research tuned for AMD GPUs first.

Active chats stay protected inside a physically backed base on every rank while spare VRAM feeds a reclaimable prefix-cache tail. When burst image, video, or accelerated tool jobs arrive, an epoch-fenced PostgreSQL GPU capacity controller requests a safe shrink and waits for confirmed multi-rank release before committing the grant.

GPU-independent core: openmettle-core deploys on CPU alone, integrates optional GPU packages through Kubernetes DRA (V1-20), plans qualified NVIDIA profiles, and connects cleanly to user-configured external OpenAI-compatible backends.

V1-14 · ADR 0039 P-01

Scoped Stateless MCP 2026-07-28 & Bounded Outputs

Two-phase write intents commit a durable PostgreSQL intent before any outbound bytes leave the worker, increment fenced attempt counters under live authorization, and hold sticky send-uncertainty (outcome_unknown) so destination-authorized reconciliation never blindly repeats an uncertain write.

Outbound MCP calls run over SSRF- and DNS-pinned TLS-only transport. Model context stays capped at 16 KiB (200 lines), automatically spilling larger outputs to scoped artifacts with paginated artifact.read_range reads and downstream handle redaction.

V1-15 · V1-16 · ADR 0031 · 0040 · 0041 P-02

Traversal-Proof Storage, Volume Pinning & Retention

Descriptor-relative openat, O_NOFOLLOW, and linkat filesystem isolation (rustix, zero unsafe code) pairs with role-tagged volume sentinels and inode-verified ReadWriteMany volume adoption (openmettle storage adopt and openmettle-admin storage rebind).

Crash-safe 4-state staging/reclaim and 3-stage .purge.<id> cleaners protect in-flight downloads, alongside configurable 30-day soft-delete grace windows, operator retention holds, fail-closed restore barriers, and an append-only SHA-256 hash-chained deletion journal.

V1-07 · V1-10 TO V1-12 · ADR 0032 TO 0036 P-03

Branch Graphs, Layered Presets & Durable Runs

Conversations persist as immutable parent-linked branch graphs where edits and retries fork cleanly without erasing history, backed by a fair SKIP LOCKED PostgreSQL queue, fenced worker leases, and HMAC-signed SSE cursors that resume cleanly from any API replica.

Four-layer presets (model -> preset -> project -> request) combine provider-declared reasoning controls, qualified replay pinning, honest measured versus estimated context budgets, and pre-round token and micro-cost reservations settled to exact usage.

V1-22 TO V1-25 · STAGE 3 · ADR 0036 P-04

Scoped Memory, Hybrid Search & Document Grounding

Private personal and project memory scopes (project_only default via Graphiti, targeting permissive Apache-2.0 HelixDB) enforce explicit provenance and temporary-chat opt-out with zero cross-project or cross-user bleed.

Planned Stage 3 retrieval combines hybrid dense and lexical (BM25) search, reranking, and bounded Markdown/text chunking with verifiable span citations, provider-neutral web search (Exa and Tavily), bounded deep research, and live read-visibility re-verification on every prompt hydration.

V1-19 · V1-21 · #64 · ADR 0036 · 0042 P-05

Multimodal Vision, Voice & Burst Media Workflows

Branch-pinned image and document attachment revisions supply vision-capable models directly from scoped artifact storage without duplicating base64 payloads in conversation history, rechecking active revision state and permissions on every turn.

Curated Stage 3 adapters bring local image and video generation jobs plus launch-scope request-based dictation and spoken replies under fenced GPU capacity grants and confirmed Elastic KV Cache release.

V1-26 TO V1-35 · ADR 0010 · 0041 P-06

Hybrid Providers, Restore Safety & Multi-Client Delivery

Optional user-configured external OpenAI-compatible providers stay guarded by strict TLS/SSRF egress controls and planned envelope-encrypted PostgreSQL secrets (V1-28), alongside planned Agent Sandbox and gVisor coding workspaces (V1-26, V1-27).

Coordinated backup and restore drills (V1-30, V1-31) preserve historical receipts, replay the deletion journal, and quarantine in-flight writes (outcome_unknown) without re-authorizing duplicate side effects, while generic Helm charts, Step 2 Flutter (openmettle-app), and openmettle-acp complete delivery.
